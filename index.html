The Cybertruck's Unique Storage Architecture

No truck before the Cybertruck has offered this particular combination of storage zones. Traditional pickup trucks give you a bed, maybe a small frunk on the rare EV, and that's about it. The Cybertruck reimagines truck storage from the ground up:

  • The Vault: A powered retractable tonneau cover with integrated locking — not an add-on, but a core part of the truck's design
  • The Frunk: 2.27 cubic feet of lockable front storage that drains, sealing it for wet items
  • The Sub-Trunk: Hidden under-floor storage in the bed for valuables and emergency gear
  • The Bed: A 6-foot cargo area with built-in 120V power outlets and 12V accessory ports
  • Cabin Storage: Center console, door pockets, and under-seat areas

Understanding each zone's strengths lets you build a storage system that genuinely works for your lifestyle — whether you're an overlander, a contractor, a family road-tripper, or a daily commuter who happens to own a very impressive truck.

The Cybertruck Vault: Maximizing the Powered Tonneau

The vault is the Cybertruck's signature storage feature. Unlike traditional pickup trucks where a tonneau cover is an optional aftermarket addition, the Cybertruck vault is factory-integrated and controlled via the touchscreen or the Cybertruck app. It opens and closes electrically, locks automatically, and creates a weather-sealed cargo compartment when closed.

What many owners don't immediately realize: the vault is only one organizational layer on top of the bed. Adding bed organizers inside the vaulted space dramatically increases utility. When the vault is closed, you have a secure, weather-resistant zone. When it's open, you have a full 6-foot bed for oversized cargo. The ability to switch between modes is one of the Cybertruck's most compelling practical features.

The Frunk: Small but Mighty

The Cybertruck frunk is approximately the size of a large duffel bag — 2.27 cubic feet. That's less than what you'd get in a car trunk, but its positioning, security features, and drain plug make it uniquely useful in ways a bed can't replicate.

Because the frunk is completely separate from the bed, it's ideal for items you want to access without opening the main cargo area. Daily essentials — jumper cables, work gloves, a change of shoes, a portable charger — live perfectly in the frunk where they're out of sight but instantly accessible. The fact that it drains means you can also run it as a cooler on day trips.

Frunk Organizer Trays and Inserts

The Cybertruck frunk's floor and walls are smooth plastic that doesn't naturally organize anything. Aftermarket frunk organizer trays create subdivisions that keep contents from rolling around. The best designs fit the frunk's dimensions snugly while leaving room for a layer beneath for items you want to conceal.

Multi-compartment foam inserts are another excellent option — particularly for owners who store emergency equipment or first aid supplies in the frunk. Foam can be custom-cut to fit specific items, creating a military-spec organization system that holds each item securely during off-road driving.

  • Prevents items from rolling or shifting
  • Multi-compartment designs for organized access
  • Foam inserts for custom tool/equipment organization
  • Drain-compatible designs maintain frunk drain access

The Sub-Trunk: The Hidden Storage Gem

Many new Cybertruck owners don't immediately discover the sub-trunk — a compartment beneath the rear cargo floor that provides additional hidden, lockable storage. This area is ideal for valuables, emergency equipment, and items you want secured away from casual view when the vault is open.

The sub-trunk is especially valuable for tradespeople who carry expensive tools or equipment. When parked at a job site with the vault open to access the bed, tools stored in the sub-trunk remain hidden and secure. Similarly, overlanders can store GPS devices, satellite communicators, and cash here when camping in remote areas.

Sub-Trunk Organizers and Security Boxes

The sub-trunk works best with low-profile organizer bins or lockable security boxes that fit within its depth constraints. MOLLE-compatible pouches attached to the lid or walls of the compartment add additional organization capacity without taking up floor space. Small tool rolls and compact first aid kit bags are ideal sub-trunk residents.

For owners who travel frequently or camp solo, a thin cable lock or small hasp lock on the sub-trunk's contents provides an additional security layer even if someone manages to access the sub-trunk itself.

  • Lockable security boxes for valuables
  • Cable lock compatibility for additional security

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Cybertruck vault?

The Cybertruck vault is a powered retractable tonneau cover that slides into the bed, creating a lockable, weatherproof cargo compartment. It's integrated into the truck's design, unlike aftermarket tonneau covers on traditional trucks.

How much cargo space does the Cybertruck frunk have?

The Cybertruck frunk has approximately 2.27 cubic feet of cargo space. It's lockable, drainable, and can be used to transport coolers, bags, or other items securely away from the main bed.

What is the Cybertruck sub-trunk?

The sub-trunk is a hidden storage compartment beneath the rear cargo floor. It provides additional lockable storage separate from the main bed area and is ideal for valuables, emergency equipment, and gear you want out of sight.

What is the bed length on the Cybertruck?

The Cybertruck has a 6-foot bed, which is shorter than many full-size trucks. This is offset by the large vault compartment, frunk, and sub-trunk that add significant total storage capacity.

Can I use bed dividers in the Cybertruck?

Yes, bed dividers and organizers designed for the Cybertruck's bed dimensions are available. They help prevent cargo from shifting and organize different types of gear into separate zones.

Does the frunk drain, or will water accumulate?

The Cybertruck frunk has a drain plug that allows water to drain out. This makes it suitable for use as a cooler or for transporting wet gear without worry about water accumulation.

What storage accessories work best for overlanding in a Cybertruck?

For overlanding, consider drawer systems for the bed, a frunk organizer tray, and weather-sealed bins for the sub-trunk. MOLLE panels on the bed walls allow modular gear attachment.

Does the Cybertruck have storage inside the cabin?

The Cybertruck cabin has a center console storage compartment, door pockets, and under-seat space. Aftermarket organizers can maximize these areas for maps, charging cables, tools, and daily carry items.

How do I organize tools in the Cybertruck bed?

Tool organizers with modular tray systems, tool rolls, and magnetic parts trays work well in the Cybertruck bed. Many tradespeople also use bed slide systems to make rear-of-bed storage fully accessible from the tailgate.

What's the weight capacity of the Cybertruck bed?

The Cybertruck has a payload capacity of approximately 2,500 lbs (single motor) to 2,200 lbs (tri-motor), depending on configuration. The bed handles substantial loads well within these limits.
